## 🔬 About Me

I'm a mechatronics engineer and researcher working at the intersection of **robotics, computer vision, and artificial intelligence**. My work centers on multi-robot coordination, autonomous exploration, and human-robot interaction, with applications spanning disaster response, industrial automation, and healthcare robotics. I teach and mentor undergraduate and postgraduate students in robotics and AI at HITS, and collaborate with researchers across mechatronics and computer science on funded, publication-driven projects.

## 🤖 Featured Projects

- **Autonomous Heterogeneous Robotic Swarm Simulator for Disaster Reconnaissance & Mitigation** — Royal Academy of Engineering–funded project (~INR 50 Lakhs, ongoing), with Dr. R. M. Kuppan Chetty and Dr. M. M. Ramya
- **Decentralized Multi-Robot Mapping & Load Handling** — ROS1 peer-to-peer architecture coordinating a 13-DOF humanoid with TurtleBot3 units, using graph-based SLAM and YOLOv9 vision for autonomous mapping and manipulation
- **Autonomous Cost-Effective Robotic Exploration & Mapping** — LIDAR-based mapping, gas sensing, and PSO-based path planning for disaster reconnaissance
- **VR-Controlled Robotic Manipulator** — Unity3D and ROS-bridged teleoperation for hazardous and medical environments
- **Autonomous UAV for Disaster Surveillance** — ROS-integrated drone with image-processing for locating trapped victims
- **Automated Guided Vehicles for Isolated COVID-19 Wards** — Webots-simulated AGVs for contactless patient care logistics
